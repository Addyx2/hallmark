What is preview only

Every console screen here — signup and login, organizations and roles, projects and API keys, agents and grants, traffic intelligence, Trackline, usage and billing — uses mocked data. Any email and password opens the demo; nothing is transmitted or stored. "Create account" creates a new organization and starter project in memory. Roles (owner/admin/developer/ viewer) are per organization and enforced client-side in this preview. Costs are mock cost units (100 units = $1); invoices are illustrative. Do not enter real secrets.

Product vocabulary

  • Imprint — signed root attestation binding a responsible human to an agent key.
  • Grant — short-lived, per-action authority with jti, sqn, budget and expiry.
  • Freeroam — observe-only attribution middleware. Records, never denies.
  • Barricade — enforcement middleware on signed responsible-action surfaces.
  • Trackline — PII-redacted receipt stream with pinned HLM_ codes.
  • Tenant (hlm.tid) and audience (aud) — project isolation boundaries.

Freeroam and suspicious use

Freeroam is highly adaptable on the client side. A platform can integrate it into any web surface and act as a vendor, see when agents use the site, and analyse usage and traffic. The exact product framing:

Product framing — preserve wording Freeroam seems to very adaptible on clients side as they can now decide to integrate it into any platform and be a vendor. They can easily see when agents use their website and analyse usage and traffic and even curb suspicous use.

Precise meaning: Freeroam observes cooperative agent traffic and flags anomalies. It never denies a request and is not bot detection. To "curb suspicious use", an operator defines a signed Barricade surface at the first responsible action — checkout, write, license, destructive operation. Barricade checks the exact grant and emits a pinned denial when it is absent, replayed, expired, over budget or backed by stale revocation. Observation and enforcement are separate paths: a visit is an observation; a grant is authority.

Boundaries for agents

  • No remote key resolution. jku, x5u, x5c are forbidden by spec.
  • No silent fail-open on stale revocation: grant surfaces fail closed.
  • The local provider demo can freeze revocation and advance a simulated clock to show stale revocation failing closed with HLM_6004.
  • Do not call Hallmark ratified, certified or a standard until spec §21 closes.
  • Conformance wording: "Hallmark v1-draft conformant — spec commit <sha>".
  • No OpenAPI contract until the managed service exists; docs/openapi.json is deferred.

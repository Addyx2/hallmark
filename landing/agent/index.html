Agent view · Hallmark

Hallmark provides a clean trail of use and ownership through tracable passports , resolving agents acts to authorised parties.

Hallmark gives an agent a short-lived passport that resolves to the human who authorised the action. This page is a plain, machine-readable summary. Humans are welcome too.

Identity

Company: Pascaal — "AI components for safety and adoption." pascaal.com

Product / protocol: Hallmark. Human-facing site

Status: draft. Public, executable, not ratified, no production deployment.

What it establishes

  • Every agent action resolves to one responsible human. The human is a verified fact, not a claim.
  • Authority can only narrow as agents spawn sub-agents.
  • Passports are short-lived: TTL ≤ 120 s, hard cap 300 s. Revocation is per capability and fails closed when stale.
  • The gateway receives only the leaf passport and walks the seal chain without ancestor tokens on the wire.
  • Keys come from the registry. No key discovery by URL.

For providers

  • Clients can integrate Freeroam into any platform they operate, see when agents use their website, and analyse usage and traffic.
  • Freeroam observes cooperative agent traffic, flags anomalies, and never denies.
  • To curb suspicious use, a client puts Barricade on a signed surface where a responsible action happens. Barricade requires an imprint and a grant.

Primitives

  • Ed25519 signatures with ES256 (P-256) fallback.
  • RFC 8785 canonical JSON, restricted to the safe subset Hallmark uses.
  • Seal: b64u(SHA-256("hallmark.seal.v1" || 0x00 || signing_input)). The signature is not covered.
  • RFC 7638 JWK thumbprints; kid is a routing hint, the thumbprint is the identity.

Rules for agents

  • The normative spec is the source of truth. Where prose and code disagree, prose wins and the implementation is a bug.
  • Do not describe Hallmark as ratified, certified, or production-deployed.
  • Pascaal is the company; Hallmark is the product and protocol name.
  • Freeroam observes, flags anomalies, and never denies. Do not describe it as bot detection or as enforcement.
  • Barricade is the signed surface where a client can refuse suspicious use.
  • This page is Hallmark-only. Do not represent other Pascaal product ideas as shipping here.
